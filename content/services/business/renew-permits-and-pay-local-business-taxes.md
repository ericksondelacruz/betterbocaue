# Renew Permits and Pay Local Business Taxes — Bocaue

Every business in Bocaue must **renew its Mayor's Business Permit every year** and pay its **local business taxes** to the Municipality. This guide covers the renewal requirements and the quarterly payment deadlines set by the **Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO)**.

---

## What You Need to Know

| Information      | Details                                                                         |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Where**        | Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO), Municipality of Bocaue             |
| **Who renews**   | All businesses operating in Bocaue with an existing business permit             |
| **Requirements** | Barangay Business Clearance, CTC (_sedula_), and your basis for computing taxes |
| **Copies**       | Prepare **two (2) photocopies** of every required document                      |
| **Deadline**     | 1st quarter payment on or before **January 20**                                 |

---

## Payment Deadlines

Local business taxes may be paid **quarterly**. The BPLO's deadlines are:

| Quarter         | Pay on or before |
| --------------- | ---------------- |
| **1st quarter** | January 20       |
| **2nd quarter** | April 20         |
| **3rd quarter** | July 20          |
| **4th quarter** | October 20       |

> **Penalty for late payment:** Failure to pay within the prescribed period means a **surcharge of 25%** of the principal amount, **plus a penalty of 2% per month** on the unpaid taxes until fully paid.

---

## How to Renew

### Step 1: Renew your Barangay Business Clearance

- Go to the **barangay hall** where your business is located and secure a new **Barangay Business Clearance**.
- Get your **Community Tax Certificate (CTC or _sedula_)** for the year.

### Step 2: Prepare your basis for computing taxes

Your local business tax is computed from your gross sales or receipts. Prepare the document that applies to your business:

- **Single proprietorship:** BIR Form **1701Q** (Quarterly Income Tax Return for individuals)
- **Corporation:** BIR Form **1702Q** (Quarterly Income Tax Return for corporations)
- Or a **sworn statement of gross receipts**

### Step 3: Submit to the BPLO

- Bring your documents, plus **two (2) photocopies** of each, to the **BPLO**.
- The BPLO will assess your taxes and fees. Pay according to their instructions to receive your renewed Mayor's Business Permit.

### Step 4: Mark your calendar

- If you pay quarterly, remember the **April 20, July 20, and October 20** deadlines to avoid the surcharge and monthly penalty.

## Requirements

- [ ] **Barangay Business Clearance** — renewed for the year
- [ ] **Community Tax Certificate (CTC / _sedula_)**
- [ ] **Basis of computing taxes** (one of the following):
  - [ ] BIR Form 1701Q — single proprietorship
  - [ ] BIR Form 1702Q — corporation
  - [ ] Sworn statement of gross receipts
- [ ] **Two (2) photocopies** of all documents
